# Ember Kitchen — Catering Packages and Pricing

## Overview
Ember Kitchen offers bespoke catering for corporate events, private parties, and celebrations.
All packages include professional service staff, crockery, glassware, and linen.

## Package Tiers

### Standard Package — £45 per person
- 3-course set menu (choice of 2 options per course)
- House wine, beer, and soft drinks (2 hours)
- Tea and coffee
- Minimum 20 guests

### Premium Package — £75 per person
- 4-course set menu (choice of 3 options per course)
- Champagne reception (30 minutes)
- Premium wine and cocktail selection (3 hours)
- Tea, coffee, and petit fours
- Dedicated event coordinator
- Minimum 15 guests

### Luxury Package — £120 per person
- 6-course tasting menu with wine pairing
- Champagne and canapé reception (1 hour)
- Unlimited premium bar (4 hours)
- Personalised menu cards
- Dedicated head chef and event coordinator
- Floral centrepieces included
- Minimum 10 guests

## Add-Ons
- Additional service hour: £8 per person
- Bespoke cake: from £150
- Live music arrangement: from £500
- Dietary-specific menus: included at no extra charge

## Booking Catering
- A 25% deposit is required to confirm a catering booking.
- Final guest numbers must be confirmed 7 days before the event.
- Full payment is due 48 hours before the event.
- Cancellations more than 14 days before: deposit refunded.
- Cancellations within 14 days: deposit forfeited.
