# Ember Kitchen — Private Dining and Events Policy

## Private Dining Rooms
Ember Kitchen has two private dining rooms available for exclusive hire:

### The Ember Room
- Capacity: 12–24 guests (seated), up to 35 (standing reception)
- Features: Private bar, AV system, natural daylight, dedicated entrance
- Hire fee: £500 (waived with minimum spend of £2,500)

### The Kitchen Table
- Capacity: 6–10 guests
- Features: Direct view into the kitchen, chef's table experience, bespoke menu
- Hire fee: £250 (waived with minimum spend of £1,200)

## Exclusive Restaurant Hire
The entire restaurant can be hired exclusively for events of 80–120 guests.
- Available Sunday to Thursday only.
- Minimum spend: £15,000.
- Enquire at least 6 weeks in advance.

## Event Types We Host
- Corporate dinners and client entertainment
- Birthday and anniversary celebrations
- Wedding rehearsal dinners
- Product launches and press events
- Team building dining experiences

## AV and Technical Equipment
- 65" 4K display screen (The Ember Room)
- HDMI and wireless presentation connectivity
- Microphone and PA system
- High-speed Wi-Fi

## Booking Process
1. Submit an enquiry via events@emberkitchen.co.uk or call +44 20 7946 0001.
2. Receive a personalised proposal within 48 hours.
3. Confirm with a 25% deposit.
4. Finalise menu and guest numbers 7 days before the event.

## Cancellation
- More than 30 days before: full deposit refund.
- 14–30 days before: 50% deposit refund.
- Less than 14 days before: no refund.
