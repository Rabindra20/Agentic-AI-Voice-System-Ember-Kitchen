# Ember Kitchen — General Information

## About Us
Ember Kitchen is a premium contemporary British restaurant located in the heart of London. 
We specialise in seasonal tasting menus and à la carte dining, celebrating the finest British produce 
with modern culinary techniques.

Smart casual. We ask guests to avoid sportswear and flip-flops.

Valet parking is available Thursday to Sunday from 18:00.

## Accessibility

Please inform us of any accessibility requirements when booking.
