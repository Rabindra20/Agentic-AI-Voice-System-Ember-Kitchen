# Ember Kitchen — Reservation Policy

## Booking
- Reservations can be made by phone, online, or via our AI assistant DINEAI.
- We accept bookings up to 90 days in advance.
- A credit card is required to secure all reservations of 4 or more guests.

## Confirmation
- All reservations receive a confirmation by SMS and email within 15 minutes.
- Please quote your reservation ID when contacting us about an existing booking.

## Cancellation Policy
- Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the reservation: no charge.
- Cancellations made 24–48 hours before: 50% of the estimated bill per person.
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours before, or no-shows: full estimated bill per person.
- For groups of 8 or more, 72 hours notice is required to avoid charges.

## Amendments
- Amendments to party size or time are accepted up to 24 hours before the reservation.
- Amendments less than 24 hours before are subject to availability.

## Late Arrivals
- We hold tables for 15 minutes past the reservation time.
- Please call us if you are running late.

## Large Groups
- Groups of 8 or more require a set menu or pre-order.
- A 12.5% discretionary service charge is added to all bills.

## Deposits
- A £20 per person deposit is required for groups of 10 or more.
- Deposits are fully refundable with 72 hours notice.
