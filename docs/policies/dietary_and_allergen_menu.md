# Ember Kitchen — Dietary and Allergen Menu

## Our Commitment
Ember Kitchen takes dietary requirements and food allergies extremely seriously.
All dietary information is communicated to our kitchen team before service.

## The 14 Major Allergens We Track
1. Celery
2. Cereals containing gluten (wheat, rye, barley, oats)
3. Crustaceans (prawns, crabs, lobster)
4. Eggs
5. Fish
6. Lupin
7. Milk (dairy)
8. Molluscs (mussels, oysters, squid)
9. Mustard
10. Peanuts
11. Sesame
12. Soybeans
13. Sulphur dioxide and sulphites
14. Tree nuts (almonds, hazelnuts, walnuts, cashews, pecans, pistachios)

## Dietary Options Available
- **Vegan**: Full vegan tasting menu available with 48 hours notice.
- **Vegetarian**: Vegetarian options on all menus.
- **Gluten-free**: Gluten-free alternatives available for most dishes.
- **Dairy-free**: Dairy-free options available; please inform us when booking.
- **Halal**: Halal-certified meat available on request with 48 hours notice.
- **Kosher**: We are unable to offer certified Kosher meals.
- **Nut-free**: We can prepare nut-free dishes; however, nuts are present in our kitchen.

## How to Inform Us
Please inform us of all dietary requirements and allergies at the time of booking.
You may update requirements up to 24 hours before your reservation by calling us or using DINEAI.

## Cross-Contamination
Our kitchen handles all 14 major allergens. While we take every precaution, 
we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment.
Guests with severe allergies should inform their server on arrival.

## Children's Menu
Our children's menu is available for guests under 12. 
All allergen information applies equally to the children's menu.
