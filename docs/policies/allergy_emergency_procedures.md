# Ember Kitchen — Allergy Emergency Procedures

## Purpose
This document outlines the procedures for handling allergic reactions and medical emergencies 
on Ember Kitchen premises. All staff are trained in these procedures annually.

## Prevention
- All guest dietary requirements and allergies are recorded at the time of booking.
- Kitchen team is briefed on all allergen requirements before each service.
- Dishes containing major allergens are clearly marked on menus.
- Separate preparation areas and utensils are used for severe allergy orders.

## Recognising an Allergic Reaction
Symptoms of an allergic reaction may include:
- Hives, rash, or skin flushing
- Swelling of the lips, tongue, or throat
- Difficulty breathing or wheezing
- Nausea, vomiting, or abdominal pain
- Dizziness or loss of consciousness

Anaphylaxis is a severe, life-threatening allergic reaction requiring immediate action.

## Emergency Response Steps

### Step 1 — Call for Help
Immediately alert the nearest staff member and the duty manager.
Do not leave the guest alone.

### Step 2 — Call Emergency Services
Call 999 immediately if the guest shows signs of anaphylaxis or difficulty breathing.
Provide the address: 42 Ember Lane, Mayfair, London W1K 4AB.

### Step 3 — Administer Epinephrine (if available)
If the guest has their own auto-injector (e.g. EpiPen), assist them in using it.
Ember Kitchen maintains two EpiPens in the first aid kit located at the host stand.
Only administer if the guest is unable to do so themselves and you are trained.

### Step 4 — Position the Guest
- If conscious and breathing: sit upright to ease breathing.
- If unconscious: place in recovery position.
- Do not give food or drink.

### Step 5 — Document the Incident
Record the guest's name, the suspected allergen, symptoms, and actions taken.
Complete an incident report form (located in the manager's office) within 24 hours.

### Step 6 — Follow Up
The duty manager must contact the guest within 48 hours.
All allergy incidents must be reported to the Head Chef and General Manager.

## DINEAI Emergency Escalation
If a caller reports an allergic reaction or medical emergency via phone:
- DINEAI will immediately transfer the call to the emergency queue.
- A Connect Case is automatically created with the guest's name and details.
- The on-duty manager is notified by SMS.
- Do not attempt to provide medical advice over the phone.

## Staff Training
All front-of-house staff complete annual allergen awareness training.
First aid certification is required for all supervisors and managers.
